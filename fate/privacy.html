Fate – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

EnglishDansk

Who we are

Fate is a game published by Voxaga Studios ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the purposes described here. Contact: nordahlsoftware@gmail.com.

Short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device.
  • The Android app shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The Android app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example how long a run lasted) to improve the game. They contain no identifiers and cannot be linked to you.
  • The web version (played in a browser) contains no ads and no purchases and sends no statistics.

Advertising and consent

Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one (for example to continue a run). Google AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies and your device's advertising ID. Read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google is shown when the app starts. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the game's main menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

If you buy "Remove ads", rewards are granted without showing an ad, so no further ad requests are made for them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We only receive confirmation of which product was bought so that we can unlock it.

Permissions

The Android app uses internet and network-state access to load ads and talk to Google Play, and the advertising ID permission used by Google's ad SDK. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

How long data is kept

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data (in the web version: until you clear your browser's site data). Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Anonymous usage statistics are kept by Aptabase for up to 5 years.

Children

Fate is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly process children's personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (via Privacy settings), and to data portability. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled in the app or directly with Google, but you are always welcome to contact us at nordahlsoftware@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark, that is Datatilsynet.

Changes

We will update this page if the game changes how data is processed, and change the effective date above. Important changes will also be announced in the game's store listing.

Reklamer og samtykke

Reklamer vises kun, når du selv vælger at se en (f.eks. for at fortsætte et run). Google AdMob kan bruge cookies og lignende teknologier samt din enheds reklame-id. Læs hvordan Google bruger data på policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites og Googles privatlivspolitik på policies.google.com/privacy.

I EU/EØS, Storbritannien og Schweiz vises en samtykkeformular fra Google, når appen starter. Du kan altid ændre dit valg under Privacy settings i spillets hovedmenu. Du kan også nulstille eller slette dit reklame-id i Androids indstillinger (Indstillinger → Google → Annoncer, eller Indstillinger → Privatliv → Annoncer).

Køber du "Remove ads", gives belønninger uden at vise en reklame, så der foretages ingen reklameforespørgsler for dem.

Køb

Køb i appen behandles af Google Play under Googles privatlivspolitik. Vi modtager kun en bekræftelse af, hvilket produkt der er købt, så vi kan låse op for det.

Tilladelser

Android-appen bruger internet- og netværksadgang til at hente reklamer og tale med Google Play samt reklame-id-tilladelsen, som Googles reklame-SDK bruger. Den tilgår ikke dine kontakter, billeder, kamera, mikrofon eller præcise placering.

Opbevaring

Spilfremskridt bliver på din enhed, indtil du afinstallerer spillet eller sletter dets data (i webversionen: indtil du rydder browserens webstedsdata). Data, som Google behandler, opbevares efter Googles politikker. Anonym brugsstatistik opbevares hos Aptabase i op til 5 år.

Børn

Fate er ikke rettet mod børn under 13 år (eller den minimumsalder, der gælder i dit land). Vi behandler ikke bevidst børns personoplysninger. Tror du, at et barn har afgivet personoplysninger, så kontakt os, og vi hjælper med at få dem fjernet.

Dine rettigheder

Efter GDPR har du ret til indsigt, berigtigelse og sletning, ret til at gøre indsigelse, ret til at trække dit samtykke tilbage når som helst (via Privacy settings) og ret til dataportabilitet. Da vi ikke selv opbevarer personoplysninger, håndteres de fleste anmodninger bedst i appen eller direkte hos Google, men du er altid velkommen til at kontakte os på nordahlsoftware@gmail.com. Du kan også klage til Datatilsynet.

Ændringer

Vi opdaterer denne side, hvis spillet ændrer, hvordan data behandles, og retter ikrafttrædelsesdatoen øverst. Væsentlige ændringer annonceres også i spillets butiksbeskrivelse.
